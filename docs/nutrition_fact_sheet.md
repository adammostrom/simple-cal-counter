### General Info:

Calories = Energy
Daily value = calculated based on a daily intake of 2000 calories.
The higher the daily value percentage, the more of that it fills your daily calorie intake.

Example: Crackers, 6% Fat = 6% fat contribution to your daily 2k calories diet.
